About

I'm a gameplay programmer working in Unity and Unreal. I like building the systems underneath a game, like abilities, AI and combat, and making them feel right to play.

I studied Game Development at IADE in Lisbon, with an Erasmus semester at Cologne Game Lab in Germany. Based in Lisbon, Portugal.

Alongside game development I teach it. Explaining a system to a room of teenagers is the fastest way I know to find out whether I really understand it.

What I built

Abilities with the Gameplay Ability System

Implemented limb abilities in GAS, including knockback and a distance-based stun, built from Gameplay Effects and Tags.

C++ · GAS · Gameplay Effects & Tags

Multiplayer HUD

Reworked and maintained the in-game UI: a dynamic display of each player's equipped limbs, radial cooldowns and health bars, kept in sync for up to 4 players.

UMG · C++ · Replication

About the game

Abomifighters is a multiplayer arena fighter built on Lyra in Unreal Engine 5. Players pick up limbs to fight each other, and each limb brings its own skill, so you build your character as the match goes. The in-house MVP was shown at events and to publishers.

A top-down roguelite shooter built around 60+ abilities that combine into synergies.

Gameplay Programmer
Team
2 programmers

Result
GameJamPlus 22/23 Grand Final
Tech

Links
Play on itch.io

What I built

Data-driven skill system

Architected the skill system behind 60+ abilities. Abilities are composed from modular effects, so new ones are mostly data, and they combine at runtime into synergies.

C# · ScriptableObjects · Modular effects

Performance with 100+ enemies

Optimised rendering and particle systems so the game stays stable with over a hundred enemies and their effects on screen at once.

Unity Profiler · Particle systems · Rendering

Ability design & balancing

Designed and balanced abilities alongside the programming, wrote the project documentation, and integrated all the game's assets.

Game design · Balancing · Documentation

About the game

Slimando started as a GameJamPlus 22/23 entry and qualified for the Grand Final. It's a top-down roguelite shooter where you build up a set of abilities over a run, and the fun comes from how they stack and combine. It's playable for free on itch.io.
